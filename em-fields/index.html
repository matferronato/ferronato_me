What the colours mean

Near field falls off as 1/R² and rides along with the charge. It carries no energy away.

Radiation falls off as 1/R and exists only while the charge accelerates. Because it dies more slowly with distance, it is all that survives far away.

The model
E = Ev + Ea
Ev ∝ (1−β²)(n−β) / κ³R²
Ea ∝ n×[(n−β)×β̇] / cκ³R

Everything on the right is read at the retarded time tr solved from tr = t − |x − p(tr)|/c. A two-dimensional teaching model, not a Maxwell solver.
